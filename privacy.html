Home Harmony Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Lotus Center for Counseling and Wellness LLC · Contact: admin@lotus-centered.com

Home Harmony is a household planner. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, first name and an account ID, used to sign you in.
  • Household content you enter: names, ages and availability of household members, tasks, chores, meal plans, grocery lists, laundry, calendar events, service providers, inventory, appliance records, bills and expenses, and lists.
  • Phone calendar (optional): if you turn on calendar sync, the app reads events from the calendars you choose on your phone to show them in Home Harmony, and adds your household's events to a calendar you pick. Events read from your phone stay on that phone; they are not uploaded to our servers or shared with your household. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • Family meeting notes, reward requests, fitness classes and activity minutes you enter are saved as household content and visible to your household members. Home Harmony does not read health or fitness data from your phone, watch or other apps.
  • Family photos and encouragement messages: photos you add are shrunk on your phone and stored with your household, visible to household members only. Encouragement messages are visible to everyone in your household.
  • Parent care information: medicines, doctors, visits and notes you enter about a relative are stored with your household's private records and are visible to adult members only. Home Harmony is an organizer, not a medical or monitoring service.
  • Family assistant and flyer reader (optional): only when an adult asks the assistant a question or snaps a flyer, the app sends that question or photo, plus a short summary of the household's next two weeks (names, events, tasks, meals, shopping list and low supplies; never bills, records or parent care notes), to our server, which passes it to Anthropic's Claude API to produce suggestions. We don't use it for advertising. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms and does not use it to train its models by default. Suggestions are only added to your household if you tap Add.
  • Event guests: when a household shares an invite link, guests can reply without an account. We store the name, reply, headcount, food needs, note and potluck choice they enter, plus a temporary anonymous sign-in ID so they can change their reply later. This is visible to the host household and to anyone with that invite link, and is deleted when the host deletes the event or the guest removes their reply.
  • Reminder settings are stored only on your device. Reminders are scheduled on your device and are not sent through our servers.
  • Subscription status: when you subscribe, Apple or Google processes the payment. We never see your card details. Our subscription service provider, RevenueCat, receives your account ID and purchase receipts so we can tell whether your household has an active subscription.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or payment details, and we only see photos you choose to add. The app contains no ads and no third-party tracking.

How it's stored and shared

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) so it can sync between the devices of people in your household. Purchase records are processed by Apple, Google and RevenueCat. Only members you invite to your household can see its content. Bills, budgets and appliance records are visible only to adult members. When an adult uses the optional family assistant or flyer reader, that request is processed by Anthropic as described above. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Children

Home Harmony accounts are for people 13 and older. Younger children can use the parent-controlled kid view on a parent's or shared device; their names, chores and routines are entered by the parent. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 to create their own accounts. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at admin@lotus-centered.com.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Settings & backup → Family sharing → Delete account. This deletes your account immediately. If you are the last member of your household, all household data is deleted too. You can also request deletion by email. Deleted data is removed from our live database right away and from Google's backups on their normal schedule.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date.

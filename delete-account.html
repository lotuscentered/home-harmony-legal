Delete your Home Harmony account

Home Harmony · by Lotus Center for Counseling and Wellness LLC

To request deletion by email, write to admin@lotus-centered.com from the email address you use to sign in, with the subject “Delete my Home Harmony account”. We'll delete your account and confirm within 7 days. You don't need to reinstall the app.

Or delete it in the app

Open Settings & backup → Family sharing → Delete account and enter your password.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email, name, account ID).
  • Your membership in your household.
  • If you are the last member: all household data (tasks, meals, lists, calendar, providers, inventory, records, bills).

What is kept

If other people remain in your household, the shared household content stays with them. Nothing else is kept. See the privacy policy.
